 {\bf\textit{Assignment:}} due \dueDay, \csname dateWeek03\endcsname, \HWdueTime, via Gradescope (\gradescopeCode):

\rule{6.8in}{1pt}\vskip.1in

\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove that there do not exist integers $a$, and $b$ such that $21a + 30b = 1$.
\item Prove that there are no integer solutions to the equation $x^2 - y^2 = 2$.
% \item Prove that if $a$ is rational and $b$ is irrational, then $ab$ is irrational.
\item Let $a, b, c$ be integers satisfying $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. Show that at least one of $a$, $b$, or $c$ is even. % (Harder problem, just for fun: show that $a$ or $b$ must be even.)
\item Suppose that $a,b \in \mathbb{Z}$. Prove that $a^2 - 4b \neq 2$.
\item Suppose that $x$ is a real number such that $0 \leq x \leq \pi/2$. Prove that $\sin x + \cos x \geq 1$. (Hint: at some point in your proof, use that $(\sin x)^2 + (\cos x)^2 = 1$.)
\item Prove that $2^{1/3}$ is irrational.
\item Prove that $\log_{10} 7$ is irrational.
\item Suppose that $a$ and $n$ are integers that are both at least 2. Suppose that $a^n -1$ is prime.
  
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Prove that $a = 2$.
   \item Prove that $n$ is a prime. % (Primes of the form $2^n - 1$ are called Mersenne primes.)
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
